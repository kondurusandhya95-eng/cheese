Where It Began

A Small Shop,
an Enormous Dream

Maison Fromagerie was born in 2011 from a single cave, a single family, and a conviction that industrialised cheese had gone too far. Our founders — Thomas and Élise Moreau — left careers in Parisian hospitality to return to Thomas's family dairy in the Jura highlands, armed with nothing but a modest inheritance, obsessive curiosity, and twelve wheels of hand-pressed Comté.

What started as a weekend market stall became a full cave-aging programme within two years, then a wholesale partnership with three Michelin-starred restaurants, then the shop you see today. Every decision we have made has followed the same rule: does this make the cheese better, or does it make our lives easier? The answer determines everything.

"We don't sell cheese. We sell time — the months and seasons and turning cycles that transform humble milk into something worth remembering."

— Thomas Moreau, Co-Founder

What We Stand For

Principles, Not Policies

🌿

Radical Traceability

We refuse anonymous sourcing. Before any cheese enters our range, we verify the farm, the breed of animal, the seasonal diet, and the ageing conditions. If we can't name it, we don't sell it.

⏳

Slowness as Standard

Industrial cheese is fast. We are not. We never accelerate ripening with heat or additives. Every wheel ages on its own schedule — weeks, months, years — until it is ready, not until it is profitable.

🤝

Fair Partnerships

We pay above-market prices to every dairy we work with. Small producers deserve margins that let them farm well, not just cheaply. Sustainable food starts with sustainable livelihoods for the people who make it.

🧪

Rigorous Quality

Every wheel is tasted blind before dispatch. Our head cheese grader rejects an average of 8% of each batch. Customers never see the rejections — because they should never have to.

🌍

Environmental Accountability

We offset all freight emissions, prioritise suppliers within 800km, and are working toward fully plastic-free packaging by 2026. Our caves run on 100% renewable energy.

📖

Education First

Every order ships with tasting notes, origin stories, and pairing suggestions written by our team — not generated by AI. We believe the best meal is one you understand deeply.

A Decade of Dedication

Our Journey

2011

First Wheel

Thomas and Élise press the first batch of Comté from the family herd in Jura, selling at the Saturday market.

2013

The Cave Opens

A disused limestone cave beneath the family farm is converted into a professional ageing cellar with climate control.

2015

Michelin Partnership

Three Michelin-starred restaurants in Lyon add our cave-aged Comté and Swiss Alpine Gruyère to their cheese trolleys.

2018

European Expansion

We expand sourcing to Spain and Italy, adding Manchego, Parmigiano Reggiano and Pecorino to the range.

2021

Online Launch

Our curated box subscription launches, bringing cave-aged selections to customers across Europe and South Asia.

2024

Best Artisan Shop

Awarded Best Artisan Shop at the Lyon Gastronomy Awards and Affineur of the Year at the International Cheese Salon, Paris.

How We Choose

Every Farm
Earns Its Place

We reject over 70% of farms that apply to supply us. Not because we are difficult, but because our standards are non-negotiable. Before any supplier joins our network, a member of our team visits in person — no exceptions.

👁️
On-Site Visits Every prospective supplier receives an unannounced farm visit from our sourcing team before any contract is signed.
🐄
Animal Welfare Standards Pasture access, feed quality, and herd health records are reviewed annually. Any deterioration means a contract review.
⚖️
Transparent Pricing We pay farmers a minimum of 20% above commodity rates and publish our payment structure on request.
📋
Annual Recertification No supplier stays in our network on reputation alone. Every relationship is reviewed and renewed each year.
