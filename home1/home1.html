Our Heritage

Our Craft,
Our Story

— depuis 2011

Deep in the heart of Lyon, our caves hold secrets passed down through three generations. We source only from small family dairies — places where the animals graze on alpine meadows and the cheesemaker knows every wheel by name.

Every board we build is a conversation between terroir, time, and craft. We believe the finest food asks nothing of you except to slow down and savour.
