Philosophy

The Curation Ethos

We apply the "Cure-to-Canvas" theory. Every board is a visual and textural narrative of the landscape, the breed of the animal, and the master butcher's specific recipe.

Just as a gallery curator arranges a collection, we visit every partner salumeria to ensure each slice carries the memory of its origin and the integrity of the craft.

The Art of Assembly

Where precision transforms premium ingredients into a cohesive masterpiece. Every board is architected by hand to guide the taster through a specific sensory journey.

01

Selection

Choosing meats based on their fat-to-protein ratio and aging profile, ensuring a balance between silky textures and robust, spicy barks.

02

Mapping

Designing the flow of the board. We place contrasting flavors in proximity to create "palate resets" between rich, heavy selections.

03

Sculpting

The final aesthetic touch. Hand-folding ribbons of prosciutto and arranging sculptural clusters of fruit to create depth and visual movement.

Synergy

Synergetic Pairings

Our sommelier and head charcutier have mapped the precise intersections between cured proteins and their ideal accompaniments.

Sparkling Acids

Champagne or crisp Lambrusco that cuts through the rich, buttery lipids of high-fat salamis. The effervescence acts as a palate cleanser for the next bite.

Sommelier curated — quarterly updated

Sharp Alliums

Pickled red onions or cornichons provide the necessary acid spike to balance the deep umami of air-dried beef bresaola or wild boar prosciutto.

House preserves — small batch only

Stone Fruit

Dried apricots or fresh figs provide a floral, chewy counterpoint to the crystalline texture of aged meats and sharp, accompanying cheeses.
