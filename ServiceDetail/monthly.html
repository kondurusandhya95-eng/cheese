How It Works

The Art of the Arrival

From our caves to your table, we maintain an unbroken chain of care. Every box is hand-packed in our Lyon studio to ensure absolute integrity.

01

Curation

Our Master Affineurs taste every batch, selecting only the wheels that have reached their peak microbial succession for that month's theme.

02

Preparation

Items are hand-cut and vacuum-sealed in our climate-controlled facility, preserving the delicate moisture levels and aromatic profile.

03

Delivery

Shipped in insulated, biodegradable liners with frozen gel packs. Your box arrives at the perfect temperature, ready for immediate service.
