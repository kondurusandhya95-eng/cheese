The Cave

The Art of Aging

Cave-aging is the art of maturing cheese to perfection. In our caves below Lyon, temperature, humidity and weekly hand-turning transform fresh curds into something transcendent.

01

Curd Formation

Fresh milk is warmed, cultures added, and the curd gently cut and pressed. Each batch yields a living wheel.

Day 1–3
02

Salting

Wheels are brined or rubbed with sea salt — building the rind and drawing out excess whey.

Week 1–2
03

Cave Aging

The wheel enters our cave at 12°C with 95% humidity. Our cheesemakers turn each wheel by hand twice weekly.

1 – 36 months
04

Tasting

Our master cheese specialist tastes before any wheel ships. Only cheeses that meet our standard leave the cave.

Before every dispatch
