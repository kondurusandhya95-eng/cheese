The Memory of the Pasture

The Botanical Connection

Liquid Terroir

When a herd grazes on high-altitude alpine pastures, they consume more than just grass. They ingest a complex botanical record of red clover, wild thyme, and mountain yarrow. These essential oils are fat-soluble, migrating directly from the pasture to the milk, and eventually, into the very paste of the cheese.
