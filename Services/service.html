Our Philosophy

A Heritage of Flavour

Founded in the heart of the Loire Valley, Maison Fromagerie has spent three decades sourcing only the finest raw-milk cheeses and cured meats from small family producers across France, Italy and Spain.

Every wheel, every terrine, every cured slab arrives at our maison carrying a story — of terroir, of patience, of craft passed through generations.
